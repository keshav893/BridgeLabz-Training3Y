Ques 2. Embed a Google Map showing your school or city using an <iframe>.
Use title, width, height, and loading="lazy"



Ans 2. <!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
    <title>My City Map</title>
</head>
<body>

<h2>My City</h2>

<iframe
    src="https://www.google.com/maps?q=Delhi&output=embed"
    title="Google Map"
    width="80%"
    height="400"
    loading="lazy">
</iframe>

<!-- lazy means the map loads when it is near the visible part of the page -->

</body>
</html>
