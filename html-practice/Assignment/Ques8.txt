Ques 8. Design a Feedback Form that includes:
● A textarea for feedback
● Radio buttons for rating (Good, Average, Poor)
● A submit button
Use method="post" and action="#".



Ans 8. 

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
    <title>Feedback</title>
</head>
<body>

<h2>Feedback Form</h2>

<form method="post" action="#">

    Feedback:
    <br>

    <textarea name="feedback" rows="5" cols="40"></textarea>

    <br><br>

    Rating:

    <br>

    <input type="radio" name="rating" value="Good">
    Good

    <br>

    <input type="radio" name="rating" value="Average">
    Average

    <br>

    <input type="radio" name="rating" value="Poor">
    Poor

    <br><br>

    <input type="submit" value="Submit">

</form>

</body>
</html>
