Ques 15. Create a Payment Form with fields for:
● Cardholder Name
● Card Number
● Expiry Date
● CVV
Add the required attribute to all fields and include a “Pay Now” button.



Ans 15. <!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
    <title>Payment Form</title>
</head>
<body>

<h2>Payment Form</h2>

<form>

    Cardholder Name:
    <input type="text" name="name" required>

    <br><br>

    Card Number:
    <input type="text" name="card" required>

    <br><br>

    Expiry Date:
    <input type="month" name="expiry" required>

    <br><br>

    CVV:
    <input type="password" name="cvv" required>

    <br><br>

    <input type="submit" value="Pay Now">

</form>

</body>
</html>
