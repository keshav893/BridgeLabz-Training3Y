Ques 13. Create a webpage that includes text written in three languages (English, Hindi, and French).
Add <meta charset="UTF-8"> inside <head> to ensure proper rendering of all characters.



Ans 13. <!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>

    <meta charset="UTF-8">

    <title>Three Languages</title>

</head>
<body>

<p>English: Welcome to my website.</p>

<p>Hindi: मेरी वेबसाइट में आपका स्वागत है।</p>

<p>French: Bienvenue sur mon site web.</p>

</body>
</html>
